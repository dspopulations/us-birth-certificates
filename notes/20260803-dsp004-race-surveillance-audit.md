# Race-specific surveillance comparisons

**Original date:** 3 August 2026. Historical research note, revised for clarity in October 2026.

Fit results below predate the [September model fixes](20260905-dsp-code-review-fixes.md). They have not been regenerated for this review. Use them to trace decisions, and refit before citing estimates or intervals.

The initial audit compared model race allocations with surveillance rows treated as annual estimates. The workbook audit showed that those rows describe centred five-year windows. The earlier annual contrasts, failure labels and proposed thresholds are superseded and have been removed.

The surviving comparison requires the same five-year windows, case definition, race grouping and birth denominators on both sides. Match the observation operator before assessing a discrepancy. The [workbook note](20260803-degraaf-surveillance-workbook-extraction.md) documents multi-race and Pacific Islander denominator inconsistencies.

Pooled agreement cannot validate race-specific prevalence. A common level error and group-specific errors can offset in a pooled total. Conversely, a group discrepancy can result from denominator or definition mismatch rather than a demographic model failure.

The existing DSP likelihood aggregates age and year, rather than fitting race-specific recorded counts. Post-fit race allocations inherit model restrictions and external inputs. Treat them as conditional allocations until a compatible subgroup likelihood and source uncertainty model have been specified.

The original calibration-error scenarios used a common error plus group-specific errors. Those were sensitivity choices, not measured surveillance uncertainty. Their numerical cut-offs should not be treated as validated decision rules.

Use the [group-identification note](20260803-group-reduction-recording-identification.md) for what certificate ratios constrain, and the [current model guide](../docs/models/README.md) for the implemented model scope. No replacement race-specific fit was run for this documentation review.
